How to Use CardioAI

Follow these simple steps to perform a highly accurate heart disease risk assessment and understand the clinical parameters.

Quick Start Guide

1

Account Setup

Sign up or log in to safely save and track your assessment history in your personal dashboard.

2

Gather Vitals

Ensure you have the patient's latest clinical data, including Resting BP, Cholesterol, and FBS.

3

Data Entry

Navigate to the Home page and enter parameters. Use "Demo Data" to safely test the form.

4

AI Analysis

Click "Analyze Patient Data". The ML model will instantly process inputs and return a prediction.

Parameter Reference Guide

Chest Pain Type (CP) Clinical

0: Typical Angina, 1: Atypical Angina, 2: Non-anginal, 3: Asymptomatic.

Resting BP Vitals

Resting blood pressure in mm Hg upon admission to the hospital.

Cholesterol Lab

Serum cholesterol measured in mg/dl.

Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) Lab

1 if fasting sugar > 120 mg/dl; otherwise 0.

RestECG Test

0: Normal, 1: ST-T wave abnormality, 2: Left ventricular hypertrophy.

Max Heart Rate (Thalach) Vitals

Maximum heart rate achieved during the stress test or exercise.

Exercise Angina (Exang) Clinical

Exercise-induced angina (1 = Yes; 0 = No).

Oldpeak Test

ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest.

Slope Test

Slope of peak exercise ST segment (0: Upsloping, 1: Flat, 2: Downsloping).

Fluoroscopy (CA) Test

Number of major vessels (0-3) colored by fluoroscopy.

Thallium Test (Thal) Lab

Thallium stress test result (1: Normal, 2: Fixed defect, 3: Reversible defect).
